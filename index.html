📜 Javanese Wisdom & Quotes

"Sura Diraning Jayaningrat Lebur dening Pangastuti."

Meaning: Anger, greed, and arrogance are overcome by gentleness, patience, and noble character.

ꦯꦸꦫꦢꦶꦫꦤꦶꦁꦗꦪꦤꦶꦁꦫꦠ꧀ꦭꦺꦧꦸꦂꦢꦺꦤꦶꦁꦥꦔꦱ꧀ꦠꦸꦠꦶ꧉

"Wong urip iku mung sedhela, mampir ngombe."

Meaning: Life in this world is brief—like a traveler stopping for a drink—so make the most of it by doing good.
